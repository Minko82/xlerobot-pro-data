59.9°C

Reached at 28.2 min under continuous SmolVLA inference. +35.5 °C above ambient, and roughly 25 °C below thermal-throttle onset.

  • Measured
  • Modelled / reference

The binding constraint is not heat. Across 30 minutes of sustained load the GPU averaged 429 MHz against its 1020 MHz ceiling and sat at that ceiling only 5.5% of samples, while a single CPU core ran at roughly 92%. The workload is single-thread CPU-bound. Thermal headroom is abundant but idle — what limits the robot is the 1.00 Hz replan rate.

Percentile table

What this rate means for control. At a 1001.7 ms end-to-end pass the policy replans at 0.998 Hz. With n_action_steps = 5 each plan is executed open-loop for five steps, so the ceiling on control rate is 4.99 Hz — and every action after the first is executed against a world observed up to a second ago.

05

All three runs

Two earlier runs survive only as summaries — their raw time series were deleted before it was decided to keep them, so nothing new can be derived from either.

On the locked arm. Both run 1 sessions had the right arm (IDs 7–12) torque-enabled and holding a raised pose, with the other motor groups inferred free rather than verified. Every metric agrees with run 2 — where all 17 motors were verified torque-free — to within 2%, so a servo holding a static pose draws close to its standby current and did not materially move these numbers.

Where this result stops being valid. It holds at 24.4 °C ambient. The same +35.5 °C rise starting from a 40 °C environment lands near 76 °C — close enough to onset that the no-throttle verdict should not be carried over to a hot deployment without re-measuring.
