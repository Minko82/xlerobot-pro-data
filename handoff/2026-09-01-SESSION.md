## The thermal event — what is known and what is not

The operator saw a temperature-exceeded screen. That is the **only** evidence:

- `dmesg` covers the current boot only; its thermal lines are boot-time cooling
  device registrations, not throttle events.
- `journalctl --list-boots` returns a single boot spanning a year, and
  `/etc/systemd/journald.conf` has `#Storage=auto` with no `/var/log/journal`,
  so **the journal is volatile and the crashed boot's log is gone.**

**`jetson_clocks` was NOT the cause.** Checked after reboot: governor `schedutil`,
CPU running at 883–1190 MHz against a 1728 MHz max, so dynamic frequency scaling
was active and the board could throttle itself. The A2 sweep's
`--jetsonclocks` runs did not leave it pinned.

Last measured thermals during the run, at step ~600: `gpu@71.9C tj@71.9C`,
GR3D 99%, VDD_IN 22.2 W. That is warm but not alarming — whatever happened came
later, unobserved.

**Before the next long GPU run:**

1. **Enable persistent journald** so a repeat is diagnosable:
   `sudo mkdir -p /var/log/journal && sudo systemctl restart systemd-journald`
2. Fan on the board, not the case vents — the SoC is what reports `tj`.
3. Log `tegrastats` alongside training, as `AUTONOMY_PROTOCOL` already requires
   for Block A. Without it a second crash is as undiagnosable as this one.

## Two machine faults to fix

timestamp after the crash is therefore untrustworthy**, including the training
log's final entries. Fix before the next run or the next handoff inherits it.

### 2. Re-measure the per-joint bias

This is the number that decides whether more data is needed. Yesterday, against
v3's own training frames:

```
                 slope   intercept   mean err
shoulder_pan     1.038     +0.98      +0.99
shoulder_lift    1.019     +1.56      +2.76
elbow_flex       1.060     -3.90      -2.76
```

Slopes near 1.0 with a non-zero intercept means the policy tracks the bottle and
simply aims off — underfitting, not a perception failure. **If v6 brings the
reach joints under ~1.0, 49 episodes was enough and the hand-tuned offsets come
off. If they are still ~2.8 with 2.5x the data and 2x the steps, more data was
never the answer** and the next suspect is the architecture or the
demonstrations, not the dataset size.
