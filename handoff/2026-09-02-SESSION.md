## Things learned that are not in the diagnosis

- `lerobot-train` rejects `--dataset.image_transforms.tfs.affine.weight=...`;
  enabling transforms turns on ±5° rotation and 5 % translation, which a fixed
  camera policy must not see. v8 trains without augmentation.
- The Mac decodes the AV1 datasets fine with `av` 15 directly; `LeRobotDataset`
  on the Mac is still broken (torchvision video reader), so decode with `av`
  and read parquet with pandas (`scripts/freeze_dataset_videos.py` does).
- `bottle_map.py`'s dark-blob centroid misses 5/49 episodes and predicts lift
  to only ±21 units; the cap detector in `diag_2026-09-02/` is better for pan,
  no better for depth, because the operator used two grasp styles.
